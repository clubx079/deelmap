

## Deelmap Buyer Marketplace

**Marketplace — trust & quality**

- Buyer Portal Dashboard: "Recently viewed" now hides deals that have since been taken down, so you never open a dead listing.
- Marketplace: Sold or expired deals now clearly show a **"Sold"** tag with contact turned off.
- Market Insights: now has clickable states and a 30-day price-trend indicator.

**Messaging & offers**

- **Chat upgrades** — real blocking (a blocked person can no longer message you), per-message actions (reply, copy, delete, report), and a fixed unread-message counter that used to be stuck at zero.

**Account security**

- Closed a loophole where listing add-ons could be granted without a confirmed payment.
- Contracts can now only be deleted by the people who own them. *Before, the system didn't check ownership — so in theory someone could delete a contract that wasn't theirs.*
- Fixed a password-reset message that could reveal whether an email had an account. *Before, trying to reset a password gave a different message for a registered email vs. an unknown one, which someone could use to figure out who already has an account.*

**Community**

- Built **"one hub"** — your saved posts and notifications now live in one place, and community alerts appear in the main notification bell.
- Added a **sub-menu at the top** (Profile · Saved · Notifications) so you can move between those pages in one tap, and a **"View your profile" shortcut** in the side panel.
- **One handle everywhere** — the same handle is now both your community name and the name shown across the portal.
- **New: topic subscriptions** — subscribe to the topics ("Lots") you care about and get a notification *and* an email whenever someone posts in them.
- Fixed a **spacing issue at the top** where content was getting hidden behind the menu bar.
- Cleaned up the community look to match our brand guidelines (colors, spacing, rounded corners, button sizing).

## Deelmap Seller Portal

**Dashboard & listings**

- Dashboard numbers are now accurate (real "closed this month", correct weekly counts) and load faster.
- My Listings has **bulk actions, clear status labels, and view counts**; scraped deals stay off until you choose to publish them.
- Analytics now **export to CSV and PDF** — account stats, traffic sources, top listings, and the buyers interested in each property.
- Edit Listing is more reliable — safer photo swapping, accurate save status, correct bed/bath labels.

**Messaging & offers**

- Sellers can **respond to buyer offers without leaving the inbox**, and can **unblock** buyers they'd previously blocked.

**Smaller improvements**

- Renew add-ons, a clear "past due" billing prompt, a working emoji picker, a delete confirmation on contracts, and a notification bell in the sidebar.
